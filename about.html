About

One humanity. One responsibility.

OHI — One Humanity Initiative — was built around a simple belief: when people are facing devastating loss, distance should not determine whether we care. We bring people together to support humanitarian relief with urgency, dignity and accountability.

Our mission

To turn compassion into meaningful humanitarian support for people facing disaster and crisis, while building a culture of transparency that respects every person behind every contribution.

Trust is part of the mission.

Every campaign is intended to be backed by clear fundraising records, documented relief activity and public updates. As OHI grows, we will make our use of funds increasingly visible to the people who made the work possible.
