Gaza / Palestine Humanitarian Relief

A continuing effort to contribute to humanitarian relief in Gaza / Palestine as verified delivery pathways and campaign capacity develop.

Support over time

OHI's Gaza / Palestine campaign is intended as a continuing humanitarian effort. Contributions will be directed through verified humanitarian channels and prioritized according to documented needs, with the campaign expanding as our fundraising capacity and verified delivery pathways develop.

OHI will publish the relevant organizations, locations, dates, funding records and distribution evidence as they are verified and become available.

Transparent by design

Use this page for dated campaign updates, verified partner information, transfer records where appropriate, and impact reporting.

How funds may be prioritized

Food

Emergency food support through verified channels.

Water & sanitation

Essential water and hygiene support where verified.

Medical relief

Medical and health-related humanitarian support through appropriate channels.
