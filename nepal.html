Why the campaign is urgent

On 26 August 2026, a catastrophic flash flood triggered by a Himalayan glacier collapse struck communities along the Bhote Koshi and Trishuli river systems in northern Nepal. The disaster caused extensive loss of life, displacement and destruction across Rasuwa, Nuwakot and Dhading, with homes, roads, bridges, power infrastructure, water systems and local livelihoods severely affected. UN reporting says more than 84,000 people are targeted for humanitarian assistance, while as of 9 September at least 1,367 people had been confirmed dead and more than 5,000 remained missing. Rescue and relief operations are continuing, with isolated communities facing urgent needs for shelter, food, clean water, sanitation, medical care and essential supplies.
